### ⚡ About Me & Engineering Focus

I am an Electrical-Electronics Engineering student at ATU with an active engineering focus on **Control Systems**, **Industrial Automation**, **System Modeling (MATLAB / Simulink)**, and **Industrial IoT (IIoT)**.

- 🎯 **Control Systems & Dynamics**: Analytical modeling of electromechanical systems, state-space formulations, transfer function derivations, and PID controller synthesis with anti-windup clamping and noise filtering.
- ⚙️ **Industrial Automation & IIoT**: Designing deterministic closed-loop feedback architectures, sensor/actuator integration, industrial communication protocols, and embedded telemetry.
- 🔬 **Simulation & Modeling**: Physical plant simulation, root locus & Bode frequency-domain stability analysis, transient optimization, and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) concepts using MATLAB, Simulink, and scientific Python.
- 💻 **Software & Low-Level Architecture**: Bridging high-level mathematical modeling with high-performance low-level embedded implementations in C/C++ and Python.

### 🚀 Featured Engineering Project

<table>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <h3>🔬 <a href="https://github.com/mustafakorkunc/dc-motor-pid-control">dc-motor-pid-control</a></h3>
      <p><strong>DC Motor PID Speed & Position Control Simulation Suite</strong></p>
      <p>
        An academic-grade analytical modeling, simulation, and frequency-domain stability verification suite for an armature-controlled Direct Current (DC) servomotor under closed-loop PID control.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>First-Principles Physics</strong>: Electromechanical differential equations derived via Kirchhoff's Voltage Law & Newton's Second Law.</li>
        <li><strong>Controller Synthesis</strong>: Continuous LTI filtered PID controller and discrete digital implementation with anti-windup clamping and actuator saturation bounds (&plusmn;24V).</li>
        <li><strong>Stability Verification</strong>: Automated extraction of transient metrics (Rise Time, Settling Time, Overshoot) and Bode frequency-domain margins (Gain Margin: &infin;, Phase Margin: 84.76&deg;).</li>
        <li><strong>Disturbance Rejection</strong>: Step load torque disturbance rejection demonstrating zero steady-state droop.</li>
      </ul>
